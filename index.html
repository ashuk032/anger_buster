How it works

Three steps to calm

A full round takes about two minutes. Best played when you're properly annoyed.

  1. 1
    📷

    Make the faces

    Upload a photo for each character, or keep the cute cartoon faces. Each photo becomes a squishy square cushion.

  2. 2
    👆

    Pick & tap

    Choose a weapon and tap on him. Quick hits in a row make combos, which lower your anger faster.

  3. 3
    📉

    Watch it drop

    Every hit lowers your Anger Level. At 0% you get confetti and Peace Restored ❤️.

Cushion faces

Any photo becomes a pillow 🛋️

Upload a picture, drag to frame the face and zoom in. It turns into a puffy cushion with stitches and corner tufts that squishes, gets sooty and drips.

  • Stays on your device. Photos are cropped in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
  • Cute cartoon faces by default. Skip the photos and you still get angry, dizzy and happy faces.
  • Clear them anytime. One tap brings the cartoon face back.
🌸 Cool Down Mode

Then… breathe.

One tap switches to a pastel sky with floating bubbles, falling petals and soft music. A breathing circle sets the pace, tapping plays little chimes, and he just dozes off. Zzz… 💤

The fine print (it's short)

🧸Cartoon-only chaos

No blood, no injuries. The worst that happens is soot, cream and a bad hair day.

🔒Private by design

No accounts, no tracking. Photos stay on the device they were picked on.

📱Phone-friendly

Tap to throw on your phone. On a computer, keys 1–8 switch weapons.

🎵Bring your music

Play the free built-in beats, or paste a YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud or Apple Music link and bust to your own playlist.
